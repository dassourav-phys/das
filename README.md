
# Sourav Das
**Quantum Physicist | Doctoral Student**

Welcome to my academic portfolio. I recently completed my Ph.D. in Quantum Physics, specialising in quantum information theory, quantum metrology, light-matter interaction, and quantum light spectroscopy.

---

## 🔬 Research Focus & Interests

My research sits at the intersection of condensed matter physics and quantum information science. I am particularly interested in:
*   **Superconducting Qubits:** Decoherence mechanisms, noise spectroscopy, and hardware-efficient circuit design.
*   **Quantum Error Correction (QEC):** Fault-tolerant protocols, surface codes, and real-time decoding algorithms.
*   **Quantum Simulation:** Harnessing noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) devices for simulating complex many-body quantum systems.
